# COPD, discharge guidance for the recovery window

Source note: compiled from publicly available patient education guidance on COPD
self care after a hospital stay. Used in this prototype as retrieval material
only. Not clinical advice.

## Warning signs to watch in the first 30 days

- More breathlessness than usual during ordinary activity such as dressing or
  walking to the bathroom.
- A change in the colour, thickness or amount of sputum.
- Needing the rescue inhaler more often than usual.
- Fever, chills, or a new cough that is different from the usual cough.
- Swelling in the ankles or feet.
- Confusion, drowsiness or difficulty staying awake. This is an emergency sign.
- Lips or fingertips turning blue or grey. This is an emergency sign.

## Plan steps that commonly fail after discharge

- Steroid or antibiotic courses that are started and then stopped early.
- Inhaler technique that was demonstrated once and never checked again.
- Pulmonary rehabilitation referrals that are never taken up.
- Follow up appointments missed because of breathlessness or lack of transport.

## Expected timings used by this prototype

- New prescriptions collected within 2 days of discharge.
- Follow up appointment booked within 7 days of discharge.
- Follow up appointment attended within 14 days of discharge.
